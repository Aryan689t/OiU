# Product Brief

## Product name
the assistant (working name)

## One-liner
A meeting awareness copilot that helps people catch the moments directed at them and recover context when they lose focus.

## Problem
During online meetings, a participant may multitask, step away, or miss part of the conversation. They can miss a name call, an attendance check, a question, or an assignment. Generic meeting summaries are often too broad and arrive too late to help answer in the moment.

## Target users
- Students in online classes, seminars, and viva sessions
- Employees and teams in remote or hybrid meetings
- Anyone who wants to recover context after distraction or temporary absence

## Core promise
the assistant continuously maintains meeting context while active, interrupts only when relevant, and provides a concise briefing with an optional response suggestion.

## Product pillars
1. **Awareness:** detect a configured name/identifier or relevant meeting event.
2. **Context recovery:** explain what was said and what led to the question.
3. **Response support:** suggest possible wording grounded in the transcript.
4. **User control:** user chooses when listening starts/stops and whether to use a suggestion.

## Differentiation
the assistant is not primarily a transcript archive or a post-meeting summary generator. It is an in-meeting attention and context-recovery layer.

## Non-goals for MVP
- Auto-speaking, auto-unmuting, or answering on behalf of the user
- Meeting scheduling or calendar management
- Native desktop app
- Support for every conferencing platform
- Emotion, personality, or productivity scoring
- Covert recording
- Guaranteed identification of the actual speaker
