Engineered for Effortless Meeting Awareness

Built from the ground up to keep you present, informed, and ready to respond without stress.

Pure Audio Mode (Groq Whisper)

Listens directly to incoming speech from the meeting audio stream using Groq's blazing-fast Whisper Large V3 Turbo model. Completely independent of Google Meet captions.

0ms Phonetic & ID Detection

Instantly detects when your exact name, phonetic aliases (e.g., Tathagat, Aryan), registration numbers (e.g., 117), or custom keywords (e.g., Attendance) are called.

Instant 1-Click Answers

When asked direct questions, OiU automatically provides factual Direct, Professional, and Clarification responses you can copy with 1 click to unmute and answer confidently.

7-Second Utterance Ceiling

Consecutive questions and long monologues are neatly segmented into clean, distinct turns. Old speech is archived automatically so alert cards stay crisp and focused.

100% Private & Device-Centric

Zero tracking or third-party audio retention. Audio is processed purely on your machine and via your own secure, free Groq or Google Gemini API key.

Non-Intrusive & Zero-Impact

Lives in Chrome's native Sidepanel. Never modifies Google Meet's layout or causes screen clutter. Respects manual caption controls completely.

Install in 60 Seconds

No build tools or command line required. Works in any standard Google Chrome browser.

STEP 01

Download Extension

Download oiu-extension.zip and extract it into a folder on your computer.

STEP 02

Open Extensions

In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn ON the Developer mode toggle in the top-right corner.

STEP 03

Load Unpacked

Click the Load unpacked button in the top-left and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.

STEP 04

Configure & Listen

Pin the OiU icon, enter your Name & Registration ID in Settings, open Google Meet, and click Start Listening!
